Join us

We are always looking for new students with an interest in using synthetic organic chemistry to explore biological questions.

Graduate students

Potential applicants should send:

  • Transcripts
  • A brief resume
  • Contact information for at least two academic references

Competitive students typically have a GPA of at least 3.5 from their bachelor’s degree, along with some form of research experience.

For general information on graduate studies in chemistry at the University of Calgary, see the Department of Chemistry website.

Undergraduate students

Interested in organic synthesis and entering your fourth year at the University of Calgary? You may be interested in an honours or summer project in the Derksen lab.

When applying, please send:

  • Transcripts
  • At least one academic reference

External funding for summer scholarship positions tends to be very competitive and typically requires a GPA above 3.7.

For more on funding and research as an undergraduate, talk to your science advisors at the Undergraduate Science Centre.
