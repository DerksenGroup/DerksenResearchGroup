Our newest publication in Chemistry – A European Journal

Our PhD candidate, Ricky Tran, has officially had his newest paper published in Chemistry – A European Journal: “Leveraging Pyrazolium Ylide Reactivity to Access Indolizine and 1,2-Dihydropyrimidine Derivatives.” Check it out for a read that will interest synthetic chemists. Congratulations Ricky and to all involved!
